How this works

Powered by Autonimator.

The truck reports in. Autonimator does the talking.

Autonimator connects to the Cybertruck and exposes its drives, charging sessions, Supercharger receipts, and live status to my AI assistant through a secure API. Each week the assistant pulls the numbers, reduces them to totals, and republishes this page. No spreadsheets, no screenshots, no typing.

Powered by Autonimator
  • DrivesDistance, duration, energy, and measured Wh per mile for every trip.
  • ChargingHome sessions plus Tesla's own Supercharger receipts, with kWh billed per site.
  • Live statusBattery, range, temperatures, and software version at the moment of the update.
  • AutomationsThe same connection sets my charge limit every night based on the next day's calendar.

Honest numbers

Measured, not guessed

Efficiency is measured energy over measured miles, so short hops with no reading are left out rather than estimated.

Privacy

Totals only

Only per-day totals are kept. No routes, addresses, or times are stored or published; Supercharger stops use Tesla's public site names.

Why no dollar figure

Free Supercharging

TARS2 came with free Supercharging for life, so every kWh above cost nothing. Tesla referral link if you're buying.
