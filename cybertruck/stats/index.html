How this works

Where the numbers come from.

Source

The truck

Drives, charging sessions, and Supercharger receipts are pulled from the vehicle's own data each week. Efficiency is measured energy over measured miles, so short hops with no reading are left out rather than guessed.

Privacy

Totals only

Only per-day totals are kept. No routes, addresses, or times are stored or published; Supercharger stops are named by Tesla's public site names.

Why it's free

Free Supercharging

TARS2 came with free Supercharging for life, so the kWh above cost nothing. That is why there's no dollar figure here. Tesla referral link if you're buying.
