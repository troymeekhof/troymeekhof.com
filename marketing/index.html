What I do

Grand Rapids Marketing Co. (GRMC). I co-founded GRMC with Sam Kiste. I’m the Creative Director; Sam runs logistics. The tagline is “Brilliant Marketing for Any Budget.” GRMC is a full-service creative and marketing shop: we take on whatever a business needs, whether that’s video, photo, web, SEO, paid ads, or social. We don’t sell packaged products. What most clients keep us around for is a monthly content retainer: video and photo content produced every month for their social and web channels.

Freelance and consulting. Outside GRMC, I take on video production, web development, and search work directly. My toolkit: WordPress and custom plugins, Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, Search Console, Semrush, DaVinci Resolve, Salesforce/Jungo CRM automation, and mortgage marketing compliance. I currently handle marketing and compliance work for Treadstone Mortgage.

GEO and AI visibility. Search is no longer just ten blue links. Generative engine optimization, making sure ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google’s AI Overviews cite you accurately, is a core part of how I build sites now. This site is an example: structured data, an llms.txt file, and plain, verifiable facts so the machines get me right.

FAQs

What is a content retainer?

It’s a monthly plan for video and photo content for your social and web channels. Grand Rapids Marketing Co. plans, shoots, edits, and delivers it, so you have a steady flow of posts made by people who know how to shoot and how to market. The Found Cottage Mercantile Market is a recent example: same-day reels shot and cut during the event.

What's the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO gets you ranked in traditional search results. GEO, generative engine optimization, gets you cited correctly by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. They share a foundation of clear structure and verifiable facts, but GEO puts extra weight on entity data, schema, and being the obvious source of truth about your business.

Do you work outside Grand Rapids?

Yes. GRMC’s core clients are West Michigan businesses, but SEO and web work don’t care about geography, and I’ve shot video projects as far away as California. If you’re local, I’ll probably show up in a Cybertruck.
